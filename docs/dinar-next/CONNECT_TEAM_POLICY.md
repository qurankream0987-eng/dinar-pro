# Owner-approved Connect human team policy — 2026-11-05

The owner explicitly selected:

> قراءة الكتالوج والتقارير فقط؛ إدارة الربط والمفاتيح للمالك (موصى به).

Human Connect members may receive only `CATALOG_READ` and `REPORTS_READ`, explicitly scoped and confirmed by the business owner. They may not manage sites/connectors, import/publish catalogs, issue/rotate/revoke keys, accept agreements, approve conversions/commission or execute finance commands. Administrative financial review remains independent and simulation-only.

Reuse the canonical D4 human delegation identity, active staff membership, business scope, current version, expiration and revocation. A Connect permission mapping is an extension of that existing delegation, not a second delegation lifecycle or an action runtime. A D4 status/version change invalidates the previous Connect mapping; owner reconfirmation is required. Existing unversioned mappings fail closed.

The existing internal marketplace/D4 policies and AI authority boundary remain unchanged. This policy does not authorize publication, deployment, paid/live providers or production access.
